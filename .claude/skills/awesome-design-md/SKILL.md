# awesome-design-md

`design-md/<brand>/DESIGN.md` documents each brand's colours, typography, spacing, components and tone.

For INFOTECH, the agreed polish benchmarks are:
- `design-md/stripe/DESIGN.md`: layered product visuals, premium corporate feel
- `design-md/linear.app/DESIGN.md`: precision, restraint, dark-mode craft
- `design-md/intercom/DESIGN.md`: conversational AI product demos
- `design-md/vercel/DESIGN.md`: type and grid discipline

Borrow principles, not identity. INFOTECH's own tokens live in `src/app/globals.css`.
